Is EcoGPT Truly Better for the Environment?

Behind the marketing of 'green AI' lies a complex story of greenwashing, delayed compensation, and unverified sustainability claims.

1

Hard Facts from the VRT Fact Check

❌ AI Cannot Be Truly 'Green'

Every search query immediately consumes power and cooling water. Planting a tree does not cancel out that direct power draw on the grid.

⚠️ Incomplete Calculations

EcoGPT only calculates the electricity for your response (inference). The energy-intensive training phase of the AI model is ignored completely.

⏳ The 'Timing Gap'

CO₂ emissions happen RIGHT NOW. A young tree takes 20 to 30 years to fully grow and actually absorb that amount of CO₂.

3

Three Myths Debunked

  • Myth 1: "Every 100 questions plants 1 tree"
    The app version claims 100, but fine print reveals that for free users it often requires up to 200 questions per tree.
  • Myth 2: "Groq hardware makes it 100% sustainable"
    Faster chips draw power for a shorter time, but peak electricity demands on the power grid remain extremely high.
  • Myth 3: "Independently proven impact"
    There are no peer-reviewed or published scientific studies proving that the environmental footprint is net zero.

VRT NWS Check Final Conclusion:

Planting trees is positive for biodiversity, but it does not make AI usage climate-neutral. EcoGPT primarily uses sustainability as a marketing tool. The most eco-friendly prompt remains the prompt you don't send.
